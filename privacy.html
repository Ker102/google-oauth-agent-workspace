Privacy policy

Agent Workspace is a personal tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google account.

  • What it accesses: Gmail (read-only) and Google Calendar (read and write), only for the signed-in owner.
  • Where data goes: data is requested from Google's APIs and used on the owner's own computer. It is not sent to any other server, sold or shared with third parties.
  • Storage: sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
